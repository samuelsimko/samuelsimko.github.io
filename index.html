Samuel Šimko

ELLIS PhD Student in AI Safety

I am an ELLIS PhD student working on AI safety, advised by Prof. Bernhard Schölkopf and co-advised by Prof. Zhijing Jin. Before that, I completed my MSc in Computer Science at ETH Zurich.

My research is about making language models hold up under adversarial pressure: tamper-resistant safeguards, defenses that survive fine-tuning, and robustness to jailbreaking and prompt injection attacks. More broadly, I am interested in adversarial defenses, causality, representation learning, and misalignment detection. My work has been featured at ICML, COLM, EMNLP and IASEAI. In the past, I have also worked on projects in computational cosmology and AI for healthcare.

Outside of research, I am an avid speedcuber. I have also participated and won prizes in several GraySwan Arena manual jailbreaking contests.
